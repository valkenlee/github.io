The Essence of Hand Building in Single-Suit Hands

Pure Flush (Chinitsu) requires composing all 14 tiles of a hand using only one suit (Man, Pin, or Sou). While offering unmatched scoring potential, it presents challenges such as complex multi-waits, Furiten risks, and intense defense from opponents.
